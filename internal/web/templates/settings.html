How alike two shots of one scene must look, by what is in them, to count as one picture: they stack, and the Duplicates page lists them, even when they are reframed a little. {{.MinCLIP}}-{{.MaxCLIP}}: higher counts fewer shots as alike (93, the default, takes repeat shots of one subject but not different subjects side by side). 0 compares perceptual hashes only.
