Stacks

The most bits a shot's perceptual hash may differ from the stack's best shot by: 0 stacks only shots that look the same, higher values stack more (6 suits frames of one burst; 10, the default, shots of one view minutes apart). The card shows the best shot; its page lists the others.

Bursts and stacks are worked out again, in the background, when a gap or the similarity changes.
